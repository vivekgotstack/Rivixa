## Design references and deliverables

[Raymed India](https://www.raymedindia.com/) and [Neomedix](https://neomedixhealthcare.com/gynecology/) informed the restrained pharmaceutical presentation: clear brand hierarchy, white cartons, differentiated accent colours and simple studio photography. Their trade dress, logos and label artwork were not copied. The supplied Rivixa logo and an existing Rivixa packshot provided the brand and photographic references.

Eight original carton concepts are saved in `mockups/`, with website copies in `../../frontend/public/products/`. Exact generation prompts are in `PROMPTS.md`. Every concept is visibly labelled “PACKAGING CONCEPT”. Ingredient text, dosage form, pack counts, treatment claims, prices, manufacturing credentials, registration symbols and statutory label panels are deliberately absent because those facts have not been established.

These raster packshots are suitable for reviewing the visual direction and illustrating the website. A final print production file will require the manufacturer's approved quantitative formula, dosage form and pack configuration, final label copy and carton dieline. This document does not specify a medicine for clinical use.
